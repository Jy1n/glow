I've tried TCM before - what makes this different?
Most TCM clinics give you a single prescription and send you on your way. Our program is built around continuous adjustment - your prescription is reviewed every 2–3 weeks based on how your body is actually responding: stress, sleep, digestion, healing stage. It's TCM done as a structured, personalized program, not a one-off visit.
How fast will I see results?
Most clients notice meaningful change within the first month - calmer skin, fewer flare-ups, often better sleep or digestion too. But TCM works with your body, not on top of it. We recommend a 3-month protocol because that's typically how long it takes for internal rebalancing to settle into a lasting pattern. The first month is for you to see if it's the right fit.
Am I locked into anything?
No. You can start with the $147 trial month or go month-to-month and cancel anytime. The 3 and 6-month options are simply discounted upfront for clients who want to commit to a full protocol. No auto-renewing packages, no surprises at checkout.
I'm scared to stop my steroid cream.
You don't have to stop anything cold turkey. Our program is steroid-sparing, not steroid-shaming. Your specialist works with you on a sensible plan - many clients reduce reliance over time as their skin becomes more stable. We never ask you to do anything that risks a major flare.
Can teleconsultation really personalize my treatment?
Yes - and in many ways it's better. You can share photos in good lighting, message your specialist between consults, and get adjustments without taking time off work or sitting in a waiting room. For chronic skin conditions, the consistency of ongoing reviews matters more than the format of any single visit.
What if it doesn't work for me?
That's exactly why we offer the $147 trial month. You get the full program - consultation, custom health packs, adjustments - for less than a single specialist visit elsewhere. If it doesn't feel right, you simply don't continue. No package to escape from.
Is this only for eczema and acne?
No. We work with chronic acne, eczema, sensitive and reactive skin, rosacea/redness, recurring rashes, and persistent flare-ups. The consultation determines what's driving your specific case - your prescription is built from there.
